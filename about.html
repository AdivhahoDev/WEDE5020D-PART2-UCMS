About Our System

Revolutionizing academic management through innovative technology

The Challenge We Solved

The Department of Computer Science and Technology struggled with outdated and inconsistent methods of managing student performance data. Manual spreadsheets, inconsistent grading logic, and a lack of centralised reporting led to errors in academic standing, missed exam entries, and administrative delays.

Our Solution

UCMS is a student-developed prototype that simulates the core logic of a comprehensive University Course Management System. Our solution provides:

  • Automated grade calculation with custom weightings
  • Real-time academic standing determination
  • Instant identification of top-performing and at-risk students
  • Advanced search and sorting capabilities
  • Comprehensive subject performance evaluation

Technical Architecture

Built with modular, reusable components, UCMS processes data efficiently using parallel data structures and implements robust business logic for accurate academic calculations.
