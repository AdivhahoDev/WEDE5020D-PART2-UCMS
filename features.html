Powerful Features for Modern Education

Everything you need to manage academic performance effectively

📝

Input & Process Student Results

Easily enter test scores, assignment marks, and subject data. The system automatically validates inputs and maintains data integrity across all records.

🎓

Calculate Final Grades & Academic Standing

Automated calculation with configurable weightings (Test: 40%, Assignments: 30% each). Determine academic standing: Dean's List (75%+), Good Standing (50-74%), At Risk (below 50%).

🏆

Identify Top-Performing Students

Instantly recognize academic excellence with automated ranking and performance analytics across all subjects.

⚠️

Identify At-Risk Students

Early intervention through automated identification of students falling below academic standards, enabling timely support.

🔍

Search & Sort Student Data

Powerful search functionality by student ID or surname, with sorting capabilities by name or academic performance.

📊

Evaluate Subject Performance

Comprehensive analytics per subject including averages, highest/lowest grades, pass rates, and student distribution.

See It In Action

The system processes data from multiple parallel arrays representing:

  • 10 Students with ID, first name, and surname
  • 3 Subjects including Programming, Information Systems, and Databases
  • 30 Result Records with test, assignment1, and assignment2 marks
